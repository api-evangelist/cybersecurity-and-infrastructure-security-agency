View CSAF Summary Schneider Electric is aware of a vulnerability in its SCADAPack x70 products. The SCADAPack 47x, SCADAPack 47xi, SCADAPack 47xd, SCADAPack 470R and SCADAPack 57x products are Remote Terminal Units that provide communication capabilities for remote monitoring and control. Failure to apply the mitigations provided below may increase the risk of unauthorized access to RTU configuration through the Secure Lock functionality, potentially resulting in a loss of confidentiality.
