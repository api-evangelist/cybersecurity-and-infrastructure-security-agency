CISA, the National Security Agency, the Federal Bureau of Investigation, and international partners released joint guidance, 2026 Minimum Elements for a Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) , that updates and replaces the minimum elements for an SBOM published by the National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA) in 2021. The new guidance incorporates stakeholder feedback from a 2025 public comment period and reflects current SBOM tools and needs while preserving the core principles of the original NTIA document.
