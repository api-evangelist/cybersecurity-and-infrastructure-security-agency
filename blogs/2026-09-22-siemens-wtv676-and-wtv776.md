View CSAF Summary The products listed below contain a denial of service vulnerability that could allow an attacker to force the devices into protection mode under certain conditions. This disables remote connectivity functions (Web Access) to the devices. Siemens has released new versions for the affected products and recommends to update to the latest versions.
