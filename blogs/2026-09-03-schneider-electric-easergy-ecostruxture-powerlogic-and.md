View CSAF Summary Schneider Electric is aware of a vulnerability in the following products: The Easergy C5 is a scalable and interoperable bay controller, protection and merging unit for large and critical infrastructure electrical distribution systems. The Easergy MiCOM P30 is a family of multifunction protection and control relays designed for medium, high and extra high voltage electrical networks. The Easergy MiCOM P40 is a protection relay series for Medium Voltage, High Voltage and Extra High Voltage protection.
